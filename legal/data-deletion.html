Data Deletion & Account Closure

You can leave WearBenin and have your data deleted, without explaining yourself and without paying anything. This page is the procedure: what you can delete, how to ask, how we confirm it is really you, the 30 days we commit to, and what survives deletion and why.

These pages describe how the platform actually works, written to align with Nigerian law (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018), They are written to be read, not to hide behind. 1. Your four options

  • Delete specific items - a listing, its photographs, a favourite, a saved search or one message thread. Fastest, and you keep your account, your shop and your saved listings.
  • Correct instead of delete - if the problem is that something is wrong rather than that it exists, edit it yourself in your account settings. Most fixes take under a minute.
  • Close your account - your listings come down and your shop page stops showing, and your account data is deleted on the schedule below. Reviews you left may stay in anonymised form so that one person leaving does not rewrite a vendor's rating.
  • Delete everything, including identity data - the full erasure: account, shop, listings, images, favourites, saved searches, messages and profile details removed from our live systems, subject only to the legal exceptions in section 5.

If you want your listings gone but want to keep your login for buying, say so; we will take down the shop and leave the account standing. Say what you want in your own words rather than picking from a menu we invented.

2. How to ask

In the app, fastest. Sign in, open account settings, choose Delete account and confirm. You type a confirmation phrase so a stray tap cannot wipe your shop, you get an immediate email acknowledgement, and you get a completion email when it is done.

By email, if the button is unavailable or you cannot sign in. Send from the address registered to your account to support@wearbenin.ng with the subject Data deletion request, including your full name as on the account; the registered email and any phone number used on listings; your shop name if you have one; what you want deleted - everything, the account only, one listing, one photograph, one thread; whether you want your reviews left anonymously or removed; and how you would like completion confirmed.

By WhatsApp. If email is difficult - poor connection, no access to the registered inbox - message +234 913 688 7631 and say you want a data deletion request. We open the same ticket, but deletion is completed only after we confirm it is really you, because a stranger being able to delete your shop is exactly the abuse we spend the rest of the site preventing.

Verification. A request from your registered email is usually enough. Otherwise we may send a one-time code to the phone on the account, or ask for details only the account holder would know. We never ask for your password, card details, BVN, NIN or a bank one-time code to process a deletion. Requests to delete somebody else's account are refused unless the requester has legal authority, such as a parent or guardian for a minor's account or an executor with a court order, and we will tell you what document is needed.

4. What survives deletion, and why

  • Tax and accounting records - only if we ever charged you a fee. Nigerian record-keeping rules require accounting records for six years. Today, with no fees and no payments, this is usually empty.
  • Safety and abuse records - reports, suspensions and takedowns involving your account are kept up to 24 months in restricted form, because scam accounts re-register and history is how we recognise them.
  • Fraud and crime evidence - where a matter is with the police, the EFCC, NAFDAC or a court, those specific records are kept until it ends, and we may be required to hand them over. We will tell you if a hold affects your request.
  • Anonymised reviews and aggregate statistics - a review you leave may remain without your name if you ask; counts and averages that identify nobody.
What deletion cannot reach. We cannot delete a WhatsApp conversation you had with a vendor, the vendor's copy of your number, a photo you sent them or a screenshot they kept - that data is not on our platform. Ask the other person directly, and if they refuse, use the route in Refunds & Disputes or complain to the NDPC. Search-engine caches refresh on their own schedule; the page itself will be gone, and search engines have their own removal forms if you need it sooner.

5. If you are not satisfied

If a verified deletion has not been quickly — you can also delete your account yourself in seconds — or we are holding something you believe we should not, reply on the same ticket and it is escalated to the person who runs the platform, not to a queue. If our answer still does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, which supervises the NDPA, and you may seek redress in court; the Privacy Policy sets out your full rights and the Commission's role.

Tell us what went wrong specifically - which data, which page, which email - and we will investigate that rather than a general complaint.
