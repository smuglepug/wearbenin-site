2. Promoted listings: what is not on sale yet, and the rules for when it is

Promoted and featured listings, homepage spotlight slots and newsletter placements do not exist yet. A vendor cannot currently pay to move a listing up a page. We intend to build promoted placements, and we are committing to the rules now, while it is still easy to hold us to them:

  1. Price published in advance on this page at least 30 days before anything becomes chargeable.
  2. You confirm the amount yourself. A promotion starts only after you see the Naira figure and accept it. Nothing recurring, nothing auto-billed.
  3. Clearly labelled. Paid placements carry a visible Sponsored or Promoted label, and we will not sell the organic ranking of unpaid listings.
  4. A fixed term - for example seven days - that ends by itself, with no silent rollover.
  5. No effect on trust signals. Paying cannot change ratings, reviews, the Verified badge or how we handle reports against you.
  6. Refunds for our mistakes. If we charge you for a promotion that never ran or that we ended early, you get a pro-rata refund.

One thing is never for sale: the Verified badge. Verification is earned by a human check of your identity and shop details, cannot be bought or sped up with money, and is not lost because you stopped paying. Anybody offering to sell you a badge or a ranking is impersonating us.

3. No hidden charges, and how we prove it

There will be no fee to register, browse or contact a vendor; no fee to remove a listing or mark it sold; no fee to appeal a suspension or a removal; no fee to submit a report, a dispute, an IP takedown, an accessibility complaint or a data request; no verification, activation, withdrawal, escrow or release fee, because none of those processes exist here; and no charge that appears only after the fact.

If we ever bill you, you will get an itemised record in Naira showing what you are charged for, the period it covers, any tax added, and a reference number to quote. If a charge makes no sense, email support@wearbenin.ng with the reference and we will answer within three business days; if we billed you in error we refund in full within 14 days of confirming it. Delivery arrangements and rider fees are described in Payments & Delivery Terms, and they are never ours to collect.

4. Tax, changes and contact

Tax. Because we take no fee today, no tax arises between you and WearBenin. If we begin charging we will comply with Nigerian tax rules at that time, including Value Added Tax at the prevailing rate - currently 7.5 per cent, which would be shown separately on any invoice - and the issuing of receipts. That is our obligation, not yours. Vendors separately remain responsible for their own tax affairs and for any business registration required to trade in Edo State; income from selling clothing is the vendor's income.

Changes. We update this page whenever a price changes, keep the previous version available on request, and give at least 30 days' notice by email and on the site before any new charge takes effect. Listings posted while the platform was free are never retroactively billed, and you can delete listings or close your account before a charge begins - see Data Deletion & Account Closure. Questions: support@wearbenin.ng or WhatsApp +234 913 688 7631. Read with the Vendor Agreement and Terms of Service.
