Vendor Agreement

Who runs WearBenin. WearBenin is a trading name of an individual operator based in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. We are not a registered company yet — our RC number will appear on this page once we are, and we will not pretend to be incorporated before then. Questions go to support@wearbenin.ng and reach a person.

This agreement applies to every shop on WearBenin, alongside the Terms of Service. It is the promise you make to buyers when you post clothing here: that the listing is honest, the item is yours to sell, the price is the price, and you will answer people who are trying to give you money.

1. Becoming a vendor

Any adult with a WearBenin account can open a shop. Sign in, choose Sell on WearBenin, complete your shop profile - shop name, area, phone, WhatsApp number, short bio, logo and cover - then post your clothing. Your phone number must be one you actually answer: a shop nobody can reach is worse than no shop, and the response time we measure from your replies is displayed publicly on your page.

One account per person or business. Duplicate accounts used to dodge a suspension or pad a shop with listings are closed. If your business has a registered name, use it in the shop name so buyers can find you by the name they know. Nothing in this agreement makes WearBenin your agent, employer, partner or guarantor, and nothing authorises you to speak for the platform or to describe your shop as owned by or guaranteed by WearBenin.

2. Listing accuracy: the core obligation

Everything you post must describe the item you are actually selling:

  • Condition, honestly. Mark an item new only if it is genuinely unworn with tags or packaging. Use the second-hand and thrifted labels where they apply and disclose faults: stains, fading, thinning fabric, repairs, missing buttons, worn soles.
  • Real sizes and measurements - the label size and, where fit matters, chest, waist, length or shoe size in centimetres. Disputes almost always start here.
  • Your own photographs of the actual item. Do not use a catalogue photo without saying so, and never another shop's photograph: that is a copyright problem as well as a trust problem, see IP & Takedown Policy.
  • Prices in Naira with the negotiable flag set honestly. If a price is fixed, mark it fixed.
  • Stock and delivery. Mark an item sold once it is gone, never keep a listing live to collect phone numbers, and say whether you deliver, where in Benin City, and who pays the rider - see Payments & Delivery Terms.

A correction in an hour costs you nothing; a misdescribed sale costs you a review, a badge and possibly a police report.

3. Enquiries, deals and conduct

Answer enquiries like a business: within 24 hours on your normal trading days, and faster for a buyer who has already agreed to pay. If you will be away, mark your stock unavailable rather than going silent for a week.

Once you agree terms in writing - a price, a size, a time, a place, a rider fee - treat it as a deal. Do not accept a deposit and then sell to somebody who offered more; do not quote one price in the listing and another in chat; do not tell a buyer an item is available so they travel across Benin City and then produce something worse. If an agent or shop assistant works in your name, you are responsible for what they say and do.

Also part of conduct: no harassment of buyers who decline, no messaging people about stock that is not yours, no adding buyers' numbers to marketing lists, no faking reviews, and no arranging a meeting in a private or secluded place when the buyer asks for a public one. The public meeting points we recommend are in the Community Safety Centre.

4. What you may never sell here

You may not list counterfeits presented as genuine, stolen or robbed property, weapons, protected wildlife products such as ivory or pangolin, prescription medicines or unregistered drugs, adult or sexual content, identity documents, or anything else on the Prohibited & Restricted Items list. Genuine second-hand and thrifted clothing - including imported bales sold as okrika or bend-down-select - is welcome; presenting stolen goods as thrift is not.

By posting you confirm the item is yours to sell, that you hold the rights to every photograph and text you upload, and that your listing misleads nobody. Vendors must obey Nigerian law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018, the Trademarks Act, the Merchandise Marks Act and the Counterfeit and Fake Drugs Act, and register for tax as required. You indemnify WearBenin against claims arising from a listing that breaks these promises, as explained in the Terms of Service.

5. Verification, reviews and fees

The Verified badge means a member of our team has checked your identity document, phone number and shop details and believes they belong together. It is free, it cannot be bought, it can be withdrawn if your behaviour stops matching it, and it is not a guarantee of your goods.

Buyers rate shops from 1 to 5 stars, one review per account, and your average is shown with the count. You may not pay for reviews, write reviews for your own shop, or arrange for friends to pile on; fake reviews are removed and repeated attempts cost you the shop. If you believe a review is unfair, email support@wearbenin.ng with evidence - but we will not simply delete a bad rating because you asked.

Listing is free at launch and we take no commission, because no sale money passes through the platform: buyers pay you directly, in person or on delivery. If we later offer optional promoted placements you will see the price and confirm it before anything is charged, with the price published at least 30 days ahead in Fees & Commission. We will never bill you retroactively for a listing posted while the platform was free, and never charge you to appeal a removal or to keep the Verified badge. Your tax affairs and your arrangement with any rider remain yours.

6. Enforcement, termination and the law

We escalate in proportion to the problem: a warning for a first accidental breach such as a wrong size label; immediate removal for illegal or infringing listings; loss of the Verified badge for repeated misdescription or ignored complaints; temporary suspension for a pattern of dishonesty or refusal to honour agreed deals; and permanent closure for a third upheld IP complaint, counterfeit trading, stolen goods, fraud on a buyer, or conduct that endangers somebody. Where conduct is criminal we report it to the Nigeria Police Force or the EFCC and preserve the evidence, as described in Prohibited & Restricted Items.

You can close your shop at any time, with or without a reason, and your data is deleted on the schedule in Data Deletion & Account Closure. We can end this agreement if we stop operating the platform, and if that happens we will give at least 30 days' notice so you can move your buyers elsewhere. Accuracy warranties, indemnity, record retention and governing law survive termination.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes follow the escalation path in Refunds & Disputes: talk to us, then mediation where available, then the courts of Edo State. Material changes get 30 days' notice by email.
