Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information WearBenin collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how you get it deleted. It is written against the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the NDPR, and it describes what this platform actually does.

1. Who is responsible

WearBenin, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria, is the data controller for the personal data described here. Email support@wearbenin.ng with the subject Data protection, or message WhatsApp +234 913 688 7631. We answer data questions within 30 days and never charge you for exercising a right. This policy covers wearbenin.ng and the WearBenin app; WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook have their own policies.

2. What we collect and why

We collect only what the marketplace needs: your name, email, hashed password, phone number and area; your role; vendor shop details (shop name, bio, area, phone, WhatsApp, social handles, logo, cover, verification status, response time); listing content including photographs; your favourites, saved searches, view counts, reviews and ratings; messages sent through the inbox; reports you file or receive; and server logs with IP address, device type and timestamps. Your browser also stores a login token and preferences locally - see Cookie & Storage Notice.

What we never collect: card numbers, CVVs, card PINs, bank account numbers, BVN, NIN, one-time bank codes, contact-list uploads, GPS tracking or third-party advertising tracking. We do not sell personal data and we are not a data broker.

Our lawful bases under the NDPA are performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in keeping the marketplace working, secure and free of scammers; consent, where we ever add optional analytics or marketing email; and legal obligation, when a court order or lawful regulator request arrives. We make no automated decisions that significantly affect you - humans review reports and suspensions.

3. Who can see your data

Other users see what you publish: shop name, bio, area, phone and WhatsApp number, social links, listings, photographs and reviews. Your email address is never shown to other users, and messages are visible only to the other person in that thread.

Our service providers process data on our instructions - hosting for the server and database, email delivery, font and content delivery - and may not use it for their own marketing. Where they process outside Nigeria we rely on the NDPA's cross-border transfer rules and contractual safeguards, transferring only what the service needs.

Authorities receive data only where Nigerian law requires it: a valid court order or a lawful request from the Nigeria Police Force, the EFCC, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, NAFDAC, the Standards Organisation of Nigeria or Customs. We check that a request is properly made and tell you unless the law forbids it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, vendor and listing data: while your account is open, then up to six months after closure to settle late disputes.
  • Messages and reviews: while live, then up to 12 months after the related listing is removed. Reviews may be kept anonymously so ratings are not rewritten when one user leaves.
  • Safety and abuse records: up to 24 months in restricted form, because scam accounts re-register and history helps us spot them.
  • Accounting and tax records: six years - currently only relevant if we ever charge a fee.
  • Server logs: 30 to 90 days. Backups: rotated out within 90 days of deletion.

If a dispute, fraud investigation or court order is open we keep the specific records involved for as long as that matter lasts, and we tell you if your deletion request is affected. The step-by-step route is in Data Deletion & Account Closure.

5. Security and your rights

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored readably. Sessions use expiring tokens over HTTPS. Admin tools need a separate secret token, so ordinary accounts cannot reach staff screens. Login and other write actions are rate-limited, every write is validated before it reaches the database, and role checks stop a buyer editing a vendor's listing. No ISO 27001 certificate, and no paid security audit - no system is completely secure. Report a weakness to support@wearbenin.ng marked Security. If a breach is likely to harm you, we notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected users without undue delay.

Under the NDPA you may access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to processing, port it, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Email us from your registered address: we acknowledge within three business days and respond within 30 days, with one security check to confirm it is you. The Commission supervises the NDPA and can order compliance and impose administrative fines - under the Act up to 10,000,000 Naira or two per cent of annual gross revenue for controllers of major importance, and up to 2,000,000 Naira for others.

6. Children, transfers and changes

WearBenin is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anybody under 18 and we close accounts we find belong to a minor - the NDPA treats children's data as a protected category requiring parental consent, and rather than pretend we can verify that on a free platform we simply do not serve under-18s. Tell us if you believe a child has registered.

Because hosting, email and font delivery may involve servers outside Nigeria, your data may be transferred abroad under the NDPA's transfer rules and contracts requiring comparable protection. We review this policy at least yearly and whenever the platform changes in a way that affects your data - for example if on-platform payments are ever added - and material changes get 30 days' notice by email.
