Prohibited & Restricted Items

Some things must never appear on WearBenin, either because Nigerian law forbids them or because they put buyers and vendors at real risk. This list is deliberately blunt: read it before you post, and if your listing is borderline, ask us first.

1. Never list these on WearBenin

  • Counterfeits and fake-branded goods. No replica sneakers, bags or designer wear sold as original, no bootleg jerseys shown as licensed, no fake labels sewn into plain clothing. Passing off a fake as genuine is an offence under the Merchandise Marks Act and the Trademarks Act. Unbranded or clearly inspired-by items are welcome if you describe them that way.
  • Stolen, robbed or diverted goods. Nothing you cannot explain where it came from. Genuine second-hand and thrifted clothing - including imported bales sold as okrika or bend-down-select - is completely welcome; stolen property dressed up as thrift is not.
  • Weapons and dangerous items. No firearms, ammunition, magazines, silencers or parts; no military, police or paramilitary uniforms and badges that could be used to impersonate security personnel; no cutlasses, machetes, switchblades, knuckle dusters, tasers or imitation weapons; no explosives or fireworks.
  • Protected wildlife and animals. No ivory, elephant hair, pangolin scales, tortoise shell, python or crocodile skin without documented permission, feathers of protected birds, live animals or bushmeat. The Endangered Species (Control of International Trade and Traffic) Act gives effect to CITES in Nigeria, and museum pieces and antiquities are protected by heritage law.
  • Medicines, drugs and health claims. No prescription medicines, loose antibiotics, codeine syrups, narcotics, unregistered drugs or supplements making medical claims, blood products or syringes. Only licensed pharmacies sell medicines to the public and NAFDAC keeps the register of approved products. If it goes into a body rather than onto it, it does not belong here.
  • Adult and intimate content. No pornography, adult films or magazines, sex toys, escort or sexual services, fetish listing of used underwear, and absolutely no intimate images of any person posted without consent - that is a criminal offence under the Cybercrimes Act 2015 and we report it. Any sexual content involving a minor goes to the Nigeria Police Force immediately and the account is permanently banned with records preserved.
  • Documents, credentials and finance. No passports, national identity documents or NIN slips, licences, voters' cards, school or university certificates, exam assistance, bank accounts, another person's SIM, POS terminals, loan or investment offers, cryptocurrency trading or betting accounts.

2. Allowed, but only with honest description

These categories are legitimate and stay on the platform as long as the listing is truthful. A misleading one still comes down:

  • Second-hand and thrifted clothing - label it used and disclose every fault; never as new.
  • Imported bales and wholesale bundles - describe the grade honestly and do not show a photo better than the goods.
  • Human hair and wigs - say whether it is human, synthetic or a blend, and give the true length. Fake virgin hair claims are our most common complaint.
  • Brand items you genuinely own - pre-owned original labels are fine if real, described as used, with the receipt or tag kept where you have one.
  • Children's clothing - allowed for adult sellers; photograph the garment, not a child.
  • Plain uniforms and traditional wear - fine, unless they impersonate security services or are protected antiquities.

3. How this is enforced

We act on user reports, keyword and category spot checks, manual review, and cooperation with the authorities. Reports of illegal listings are prioritised and actioned within 24 hours, and at the outside within 48. Once a reviewer confirms a breach we remove the listing without notice, warn or restrict the account, and preserve the evidence for up to 24 months - longer if a case is open. Clearly severe cases go straight to a permanent ban.

We refer cases to the Nigeria Police Force, the EFCC for fraud, NAFDAC for drug offences, the NDLEA for narcotics and Customs where imports are involved. A credible appeal - proof of purchase, proof of authenticity, or a plain explanation of a mistake - is reviewed by a human within three business days. What we will not do is pretend we inspect every listing before it goes live; we do not, and a report is the fastest way to get a bad listing in front of us. 4. Do not ask us to find prohibited items

The rule runs both ways. A buyer who posts a wanted request or messages vendors to source prescription drugs, a firearm, a fake bag, stolen goods or a sexual service is breaching the Terms of Service just as much as a seller is. Those requests are removed, repeated attempts close the account, and where the request suggests a serious crime we pass the record to the police. Email support@wearbenin.ng or WhatsApp +234 913 688 7631 with a grey-area question before posting; a no before you post costs you nothing.
