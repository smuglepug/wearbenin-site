2. What WearBenin is, and what it is not

WearBenin is a noticeboard, not a shop. Vendors post their own listings and buyers contact them directly, normally on WhatsApp. The vendor owns the item, sets the price and hands it over. We never own, hold, inspect, ship or insure what is listed, and we do not guarantee a vendor, a buyer or an item.

No payment is processed on WearBenin today. There is no checkout, no card processing, no wallet and no escrow. Every Naira moves directly between buyer and vendor, usually in cash or by transfer at handover. Anybody who says WearBenin is holding your money is lying. See Payments & Delivery Terms.

The Verified badge means we checked that vendor's identity document, phone number and shop details and believe they match. It is a signal, not a guarantee and not insurance.

4. If you sell here

Vendors carry the heaviest duty because buyers rely on what vendors write. Describe the item honestly - real brand, real condition, size, colour and every fault. Photograph what you are actually selling. Price in Naira, say whether the price is negotiable, and say whether you deliver and to which parts of Benin City. Answer enquiries, and honour the price and terms you agreed in writing: if you accept a deposit or agree a figure in a WhatsApp chat, that is a deal. Keep buyer contact details for that deal only, never for marketing lists.

Vendors must obey Nigerian law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018, which forbids misleading claims and counterfeits. Full obligations, verification and termination rules are in the Vendor Agreement.

5. Buying, fees and moderation

As a buyer you are dealing with a private vendor, so inspect before you pay and meet in a public place - the Safety Centre lists Benin City meeting points such as Ogbe Stadium, Ring Road and bank premises. Delivery zones and the moment risk passes to you are in Payments & Delivery Terms; returns and escalation are in Refunds & Disputes.

Listing clothing is free at launch and we take no commission, because no sale money passes through us. Any future charge appears in Fees & Commission at least 30 days ahead, with the price shown before you confirm anything. We do not pre-approve every listing: we may hide, edit or delete a listing and suspend the account behind it where it breaks these terms or creates a safety risk, and reports of dangerous or illegal listings are actioned within 24 to 48 hours. If we remove your listing in error, reply to the removal email and we will look again within three business days.
